## Product Vision

### Mission Statement

Build a **production-ready, SEO-optimized corporate website with integrated CMS, ecommerce, and admin management** that positions Desnky Global Resources Ltd as a trusted, professional provider of Energy, Engineering, Procurement, Safety, ICT, and Agro-Food Processing solutions in Nigeria.

### Core Objectives

- **Establish Digital Credibility:** Present as a modern, professional company
- **Drive Organic Traffic:** Achieve 90+ PageSpeed score and top rankings for service keywords
- **Generate Qualified Leads:** Capture enquiries, showcase projects, and build trust
- **Enable Self-Service:** Allow customers to browse services and products independently
- **Operational Efficiency:** Provide admin-driven CMS for content, products, and enquiries
- **Revenue Generation:** Integrate ecommerce for product sales with order management

### Target Audience

1. **B2B Clients** (Primary)
   - Oil & Gas companies
   - Industrial manufacturers
   - Energy sector businesses
   - Government agencies
   - Construction firms

2. **B2C Customers** (Secondary)
   - Safety equipment buyers
   - Agro-product consumers
   - General procurement seekers

3. **Internal Stakeholders**
   - Company management
   - Sales team
   - Operations team
   - Marketing team

## User Personas & Roles

### User Types

#### 1. **Super Admin**
**Purpose:** Full system management and oversight  
**Responsibilities:**
- User account management
- Role and permission assignment
- System configuration
- Security management
- Backup and restore
- System monitoring

**Access Level:** 100% of system features

#### 2. **Admin**
**Purpose:** Overall content and operational management  
**Responsibilities:**
- Content management (pages, services)
- Product catalog management
- Order processing
- Inquiry response
- Staff management
- Report generation

**Access Level:** 95% of system features (excluding security/backup)

#### 3. **Editor**
**Purpose:** Content creation and modification  
**Responsibilities:**
- Write and edit page content
- Create blog posts
- Add projects/case studies
- Manage media library
- Review and approve content

**Access Level:** Content creation only

#### 4. **Sales Manager**
**Purpose:** Sales and order operations  
**Responsibilities:**
- Manage products and inventory
- Process and track orders
- Manage customer inquiries
- Generate sales reports
- Handle discounts and promotions

**Access Level:** Products, orders, inquiries, customers

#### 5. **Support Staff**
**Purpose:** Customer support and inquiry handling  
**Responsibilities:**
- Respond to inquiries
- Track support tickets
- Manage customer communications
- Provide product information
- Escalate complex issues

**Access Level:** Inquiries, customers, products, FAQs

#### 6. **Registered Customer (Optional)**
**Purpose:** B2C customer account  
**Responsibilities:**
- Browse and purchase products
- Track orders
- View invoice history
- Manage account information
- Leave reviews

**Access Level:** Shop, orders, account

#### 7. **Guest/Anonymous**
**Purpose:** Browse and explore  
**Responsibilities:**
- View pages
- Browse products
- Submit inquiries
- Subscribe to newsletter

**Access Level:** Public pages, shop

## Key Business Workflows

### 1. **Lead Generation Workflow**

```
Guest visits website
    ↓
Browse services/projects
    ↓
Fill contact form
    ↓
Submit inquiry
    ↓
Auto-confirm email to visitor
    ↓
Support staff receives notification
    ↓
Support staff reviews inquiry
    ↓
Assign to sales team
    ↓
Sales team contacts prospect
    ↓
Follow-up and conversion
```

### 2. **Product Purchase Workflow**

```
Customer browses shop
    ↓
Add product to cart
    ↓
Proceed to checkout
    ↓
Enter delivery information
    ↓
Select payment method
    ↓
Confirm order
    ↓
Order confirmation email
    ↓
Admin receives order notification
    ↓
Sales manager reviews order
    ↓
Update order status (pending → processing → shipped → delivered)
    ↓
Customer receives status updates
    ↓
Order completion
```

### 3. **Content Management Workflow**

```
Editor creates new service page
    ↓
Add content, images, and SEO metadata
    ↓
Request review
    ↓
Admin reviews and approves
    ↓
Publish page
    ↓
Schedule for sitemap update
    ↓
Monitor analytics
    ↓
Update as needed
```

### 4. **Newsletter Management Workflow**

```
Visitor subscribes via form
    ↓
Opt-in confirmation email sent
    ↓
Add to subscriber list
    ↓
Admin composes newsletter
    ↓
Admin schedules or sends immediately
    ↓
Subscribers receive email
    ↓
Track open rates and clicks
    ↓
Manage unsubscribes
```

### 5. **Admin User Onboarding Workflow**

```
Super Admin creates new admin user
    ↓
Generate temporary password
    ↓
Send login credentials via email
    ↓
New admin logs in
    ↓
Change password on first login
    ↓
Assign role(s)
    ↓
Grant specific permissions
    ↓
Activate account
    ↓
Admin can access dashboard
    ↓
Activity logged
```

### 6. **Security & Compliance Workflow**

```
Daily automated backup
    ↓
Backup verified
    ↓
Activity logs reviewed
    ↓
Failed login attempts monitored
    ↓
Suspicious activity alerts
    ↓
Weekly security report
    ↓
Monthly compliance review
    ↓
Annual penetration testing
```

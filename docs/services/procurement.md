# Our Procurement Process

## 1. Requirement Submission

The client provides the required product, equipment, material, quantity, specification, preferred brand, delivery location, and timeline.

## 2. Technical Review

Our team reviews the request to clarify product application, compatibility, performance requirements, and relevant technical specifications.

## 3. Supplier and Product Sourcing

We identify suitable manufacturers, distributors, vendors, and available product options.

## 4. Evaluation and Quotation

Technical and commercial options are compared before a formal quotation or procurement proposal is issued.

## 5. Client Approval

The client reviews the recommended product, specification, price, delivery terms, and commercial conditions.

## 6. Procurement and Quality Verification

Approved products are ordered, inspected where applicable, and prepared for delivery.

## 7. Logistics and Delivery

Equipment and materials are transported to the agreed delivery location according to the approved schedule.

## 8. Installation or Handover

Where installation is included, the equipment is installed, tested, and commissioned. Supply-only orders are inspected and formally handed over.

## 9. After-Supply Support

We provide documentation, warranty coordination, replacement-part support, recurring supply planning, and technical assistance where applicable.
